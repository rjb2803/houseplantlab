## Hero image rules

- Use a landscape editorial composition with enough breathing room for website text placed separately in HTML.
- Keep the named plant botanically plausible and the visible symptom restrained.
- Use natural materials and a believable lived-in UK home rather than a glossy showroom.
- Never render words, logos, labels, watermarks, people, hands, surreal shapes or collage effects.
- Produce a filename, useful alt text, a caption and a visual-review checklist with every image.
- Label the asset in its manifest as `ai-generated-editorial-illustration` and require human review.

## Evidence boundary

Generated imagery may establish mood or illustrate an article topic. It is never evidence of a diagnosis, a HouseplantLab observation, a product test or first-hand experience. Symptom close-ups, comparison photos and any image used to support a factual observation must be genuine, reviewed photography with its provenance recorded.
