Blog design direction board

Three researched interaction directions for HouseplantLab. This board is a comparison artifact only; it does not change the live WordPress site.

Direction 1

The Editorial Desk

Recommended

A curated magazine-style index that prioritises the latest and most useful HouseplantLab work while retaining strong routes into plants, problems and care topics.

HouseplantLab
Plants   Problems   Care Guides   Blog
Header and context Orient the reader within Blog and provide immediate global actions.
Featured editorial rail Surface the most useful or representative pieces without implying that every item is equally current.
Discovery toolbar Let readers switch from curated browsing to deliberate discovery.
Results archive Provide a dependable, indexable list of all qualifying editorial posts.
Related pathways Turn the archive into a connected knowledge system rather than a dead-end feed.
Empty, loading and error states Maintain trust when filtering or loading fails.

Best for

Scan a small set of featured stories, then refine by topic or browse the chronological archive. Readers should understand why an article is useful before opening it.

Trade-off

Requires editorial curation rules and a featured-content field.

Direction 2

The Plant Problem Index

A utility-led archive organised around the reader’s plant, visible symptom and desired task, with editorial content as the answer layer.

HouseplantLab
Plants   Problems   Care Guides   Blog
Utility hero Make the archive immediately useful for a reader arriving with a problem.
Plant and symptom quick filters Make the information architecture visible and teach readers how HouseplantLab is organised.
Diagnostic result panel Show a focused set of articles relevant to the chosen combination.
Browse by care task Support non-problem browsing and evergreen discovery.
Authority bridge Connect individual articles to the structured plant database.
Empty/loading/error states Explain combinations that have no published answer yet without pretending the taxonomy is complete.

Best for

Start with a plant or symptom, narrow to a manageable set of relevant guides, then continue into the authority plant hub or checker.

Trade-off

More complex filter and relationship implementation.

Direction 3

The Field Journal

A visual, chronological and thematic journal that treats HouseplantLab’s observations, experiments and original photography as the primary discovery experience.

HouseplantLab
Plants   Problems   Care Guides   Blog
Journal masthead Frame the blog as an evolving record of practical houseplant work.
Visual journal stream Create a recognisable editorial rhythm while preserving scanability.
Theme navigator Let readers switch from chronology to subject-led browsing.
Plant notebook rail Make original photography and plant authority relationships visible.
Archive controls and pagination Prevent the visual journal from becoming difficult to search or traverse.
Empty/loading/error states Preserve the journal’s visual rhythm when content is unavailable.

Best for

Browse visually, notice a plant or theme, open an article, then follow a deliberate trail through related observations, care guidance and the plant profile.

Trade-off

Less direct for a reader with an urgent symptom unless search and theme routes are prominent.

Recommended: direction-1 — The Editorial Desk best balances the approved premium character, the current modest but useful article library, immediate editorial credibility and scalable discovery. It provides a recognisable publication experience without overbuilding the taxonomy before content governance is proven. Direction 2 should inform the filtering and symptom-led pathways, while Direction 3 should inform photography-led feature modules and notebook bridges.
