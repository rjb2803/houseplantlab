# Plant profile revenue model

Plant profiles are designed to earn trust first and revenue second.

## Page order

1. Identify the plant and answer the basic care question.
2. Provide scannable facts and the full care guide.
3. Offer the problem checker when the visitor has a symptom.
4. Recommend no more than three products, each tied to a specific care job.
5. Explain the evidence and commercial relationship clearly.

## Product rules

- Do not add a purchase URL until the product or category has been reviewed.
- Explain why the item is useful for this particular plant.
- Use `rel="sponsored nofollow"` on every affiliate destination.
- Keep the disclosure visible beside the recommendation, not only in the footer.
- Avoid fake urgency, unverified savings, invented ratings and price claims that can become stale.

## Structured fields

Each plant supports three recommendation slots. The fields use these patterns:

- `houseplantlab_product_1_name`
- `houseplantlab_product_1_reason`
- `houseplantlab_product_1_label`
- `houseplantlab_product_1_url`

Repeat with `2` and `3`. If a URL is empty, the page shows a testing-status message instead of a purchase button.

## Future revenue layers

- Affiliate links for tested compost, supports, feeds and tools.
- A seasonal care-reminder email sequence after a mailing provider is selected.
- Carefully placed display advertising only after traffic is sufficient, avoiding the hero and quick facts.
- Original downloadable care plans or plant journals once the editorial library is established.
